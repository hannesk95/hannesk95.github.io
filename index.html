Johannes Kiechle, MSc

PhD Candidate in Medical Image Analysis

Institute for Computational Imaging and AI in Medicine, Technical University of Munich

I am a PhD candidate at the Institute of Computational Imaging and AI in Medicine, Technical University of Munich. My research is centered around relational modeling with application to medical imaging data to improve tumor downstream characterization using graph neural networks. Previously, I completed my M.Sc in Electrical Engineering at TUM and B.Eng in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Hochschule München.
